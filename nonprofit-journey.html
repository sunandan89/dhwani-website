A founder's-eye view

From a two-person idea to a ₹100-crore institution — where does technology come in?

Every non-profit walks a version of the same road. Here's that road, stage by stage — what starts breaking, what you actually need (and don't), and where Dhwani fits when you're ready. Not before.

1 Getting started · first grants

The pen-and-paper years

Two founders, one cause, a first grant or two. Beneficiary lists live in notebooks, budgets in a single Excel sheet, donor updates as WhatsApp photos. And honestly — that's fine. At this stage, process matters more than software.

What starts to pinch

  • Funders ask for reports the notebook can't produce
  • No credible digital presence when a donor googles you
  • Every data request means a weekend of retyping

Where Dhwani fits

  • Honest advice, free: a one-hour session on what to digitise now and what to leave alone
  • Simple data collection on mForm — structured records without an IT team
  • Mostly, we'll tell you: don't buy software yet. Build clean processes first.
2 ₹1–10 crore · first repeat funders

Excel starts to crack

Two or three programmes, the first fundraising hire, a small field team. Each funder wants a different report format. Versions of the truth multiply — the field's Excel, the office's Excel, the auditor's Excel.

What starts to pinch

  • Field data arrives late, incomplete, or twice
  • Donor pipeline lives in the founder's head
  • Reporting eats the team's programme time

Where Dhwani fits

  • mForm as your programme MIS — offline field collection, one source of truth, real-time dashboards
  • mGrant NUO — grant and donor management sized for implementing NGOs
  • Adoption support — we train the field team in their language, and stay until it sticks
3 ₹10–50 crore · multi-state scale

The organisation becomes a system

Multiple states, a dozen funders, a real HR function, statutory audits, and a board that wants dashboards. The question shifts from "how do we collect data?" to "how does the whole organisation run on data?" — and now compliance (FCRA, CSR-1, DPDP) has teeth.

What starts to pinch

  • Programme, finance, and HR systems don't talk to each other
  • Every funder audit is a fire drill
  • Cloud bills grow silently; beneficiary data sits unprotected

Where Dhwani fits

  • Back office on open-source ERP (ERPNext/Frappe) — finance, HR, procurement without licence lock-in
  • Dashboard as a Service — board-ready impact, SDG, and financial reporting
  • CloudSahayak + DPDP readiness — 30–40% lower cloud costs, audit-ready data protection
4 ₹50–100+ crore · institutional scale

From running programmes to proving outcomes

Government partnerships, international funders, federated structures. The bar moves again: not "did we deliver?" but "what changed, and can you show it?" Data becomes the organisation's backbone — and its credibility.

What starts to pinch

  • Evidence demands outgrow M&E spreadsheets
  • Custom needs no off-the-shelf product covers
  • Your data could serve the whole sector — but has no vehicle

Where Dhwani fits

  • Custom platforms & applied AI — outcome measurement, voice assessments, chat-with-your-data on your own programme evidence
  • Ecosystem infrastructure — public data portals and discovery platforms, like the City Finance Portal's 4,000 municipalities
  • Thought partnership — 11 years of sector pattern-recognition at the leadership table

Wherever you are on this road, start with a conversation.

Tell us your stage and your pain point. We'll tell you what you need — and just as importantly, what you don't need yet.

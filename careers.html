Why Dhwani

Why people join

Meaningful problems, a modern stack, and AI in the toolchain. You rarely get all three in one job.

Real stakes

Ship for programmes serving millions of people across livelihoods, health, education, and governance. 150 client organisations depend on this work.

AI-native craft

We use AI across discovery, coding, and delivery, and we ship GenAI products for clients.

Unusual range

A ministry portal, a WhatsApp bot in five languages, and a group-scale CSR platform, sometimes all in the same quarter.

Field exposure

You'll spend time with the people who use what you build, in the places where they use it.

How we hire

Four steps, usually two weeks

Step 1

Apply

Send a CV, or better, something you've built, via LinkedIn or email.

Step 2

Conversation

A call about what you've done and what you want to do next.

Step 3

Craft round

A practical exercise close to real work, which we review together.

Step 4

Team fit & offer

Meet the people you'd work with. If it's a yes on both sides, we move fast.

How we work

Sector-first teams

"Development sector professionals first, IT professionals later." The founding idea from IRMA, and still how we hire today.

Sector-first teams

Most of our team has worked in the sector. They know an SHG from an FPO, and they design screens for the ASHA worker who will use them.

AI-accelerated delivery

We use AI through discovery, build, and delivery, and ship AI features inside the products. Most now come MCP-ready with chat-with-your-data.

Security you can audit

ISO 27001 certified, India data residency by default, and a dedicated DPDP practice. Most solutions ship with an independent VAPT assessment.

Built to hand over

Documentation, training, and architecture your team can run without us. Affordable to buy and affordable to maintain.

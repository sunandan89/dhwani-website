How we work

Listen. Recommend. Stay.

We're in the business of solving problems, not selling licences. No product pitch on the first call.

Listen

We start with your programme, not a demo. What are you trying to change, who works with the data, what's breaking today?

  • Programme & process understanding first
  • Free first consultation, no obligation

Recommend

Then we recommend what actually fits — buy before build, open source before licensed, and honest advice if the answer isn't us.

  • Buy-vs-build assessment, contextual to you
  • Open-source first: Frappe, ERPNext — no licence lock-in
  • Active contributors to the Frappe ecosystem

Stay

Software fails at adoption, not at go-live. So we stay: training, hand-holding, and change management until the tool is a habit.

  • Adoption & change-management support
  • Documentation and training your team can run
  • We sign up for adoption, not just delivery
⌁ AI-native: we use AI across our own SDLC, and embed it in the solutions we ship — chat-with-your-data, voice assessments, document intelligence.

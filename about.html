About us

Development sector professionals first, IT professionals later

We learned the sector before we wrote software for it. A decade in, that order still shapes how we hire and how we build.

The story

Seeded at IRMA, founded in 2015

The idea took shape at the Institute of Rural Management Anand between 2012 and 2014, where two students kept meeting the same problem: extraordinary organisations doing vital work, running on paper registers and scattered spreadsheets. Sunandan Madan and Swapnil Agrawal formally founded Dhwani Rural Information Systems in 2015 to close that gap.

What began as custom MIS work became products: mGrant for grants and CSR, mForm for field data, dashboards, learning systems, and now applied AI. Through all of it, the offer has stayed the same: affordable, integrated, customised technology that organisations can run themselves.

"Our mission is to empower social-purpose organisations in their digital transformation journey by offering affordable, integrated, and customised tech solutions."

Who we serve

Six kinds of organisations, one ecosystem

Corporate CSR

Group-scale CSR lifecycle management, partner NGO compliance, and impact reporting, for groups including Amazon, Mahindra, Godrej, and HDFC Bank.

NGOs & non-profits

Program MIS, donor management, and field tools sized and priced for implementing organisations, from grassroots teams to national federations like Transform Rural India and PRADAN.

Social enterprises & agri-tech

Operating systems for organisations that run like businesses and measure like programmes: FPO platforms, producer-group tools, supply-chain quality systems, and market-linkage dashboards, for clients like bigbasket, Swayam Shikshan Prayog, The/Nudge, and NAFPO.

Philanthropy, Indian & international

Portfolio monitoring, grant infrastructure, and evidence systems for funders at home and abroad: CIFF, Aga Khan Foundation, Charities Aid Foundation, Azim Premji Philanthropic Initiatives, Wadhwani Foundation, and Dasra.

Government & multilaterals

Public portals, scheme trackers, and programme MIS at state and national scale, for UNICEF, UNDP, GIZ, ministries, and missions.

Research & consulting

Data collection, analytics, and dashboards behind impact assessments and sector research, for Deloitte, Sattva, CRISIL, and academic teams.

How we work

Four commitments we make to every client

Affordable pricing

Sector-realistic pricing and no surprise scope changes. We got to 150 clients by staying affordable.

Field-first design

Everything we ship is tested against field conditions: offline use, local languages, and low-cost phones.

Secure by default

ISO 27001 certified operations, India data residency, and a DPDP practice that keeps our clients ahead of the law.

Built to hand over

Documentation, training, and clean architecture. Success is your team running the system without calling us.

End-to-end CSR programme management

Corporate CSR · Healthcare

Landmark Group

Mobile health units, governed by data

A preventive-healthcare programme running 12 mobile health units across Tirupur, Kolkata, Ludhiana, Gurugram, Mumbai–Thane, and Jaipur. The governance dashboard follows the programme from inputs to outcomes: camps, screenings, NCD detection, referrals, and follow-ups, benchmarked against NFHS-5 district baselines.

  • 1,48,320 beneficiaries screened across 2,184 camps in FY 25-26
  • Anemia, diabetes, and hypertension detection versus NFHS-5 baselines
  • Input, output, and outcome KPIs with location heat maps
Scale: 12 MHUs · 6 cities · 1.48 lakh+ beneficiaries

Social enterprises & agri-tech

From producer groups to farm-to-retail

Social enterprises & agri-tech

PRADAN & NAFPO

The FPO stack: producer groups to market linkages

Digital rails for India's producer-collective movement. An FPO MIS portal for PRADAN, and FPO registration, a national FPO database, and MIS for NAFPO, alongside dashboards tracking enrolment, trainings, financial performance, and market linkages of producer groups.

  • Producer-group grading, business planning, and transactions
  • National FPO database and registration workflows
  • Financial performance and market-linkage dashboards
Scale: 3,000+ producer groups · 20+ FPCs
Agri supply chain

bigbasket

Quality and pricing systems for farm-to-retail

Two systems where agriculture meets commerce: QSure, a quality management system for pulses repackaging across 10 centres, and a price derivation portal computing daily collection-centre prices across 35 centres in 10 cities.

  • Web MIS and mobile app for quality management
  • Daily price derivation across collection centres
Scale: 10 repackaging centres · 35 collection centres in 10 cities

Government & Multilaterals

Public systems, public data

Government & public data

City Finance Portal

Municipal finance, made public

A national portal where 4,000 urban local bodies upload standardised revenue and expenditure data into the public domain. The result is one of India's largest open municipal-finance datasets, built for comparability and citizen scrutiny.

  • Standardised financial data model for ULBs
  • Public dashboards and downloadable datasets
Scale: 4,000 urban local bodies · Pan-India
Multilateral · Award-winning

QSure · GIZ India

Crop insurance claims, digitised

An integrated digital solution for crop-insurance claim settlement, developed for GIZ India and awarded a World Bank grant for innovation. The web MIS and mobile app replaced a paper-bound process with verifiable digital claims.

  • Claim intake to settlement workflow
  • World Bank innovation grant recipient
Scale: Karnataka pilot
Government · Health

Ministry of Tribal Affairs

Mapping health and nutrition for tribal India

The Tribal Health and Nutrition Portal: public website, data-upload panel, and dashboards mapping nutrition- and health-related vulnerabilities of tribal populations across India, giving policymakers evidence to target programmes with.

  • National vulnerability mapping dashboards
  • Public data portal with upload workflows
Scale: Pan-India

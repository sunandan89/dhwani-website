How we engage

Four stages, no surprises

AI has shortened our build times, and discovery to working prototype now takes weeks. Field-tested design and careful handover haven't changed.

Stage 01

Discover

We sit with your programme team, and where it matters your field team, to map the process as it runs today. You get a shared spec, a cost estimate, and a realistic timeline.

Stage 02

Design

Wireframes and data models reviewed with the people who will use them. For custom builds, you see clickable prototypes before a line of production code.

Stage 03

Build & pilot

AI-accelerated sprints put a working system in your hands early. We pilot with one district or one partner first, then harden the system for scale.

Stage 04

Run & hand over

Training, documentation, dashboards, and an AMC if you want us to stay. Architected so a future team, yours or anyone else's, can take it forward without rework.
